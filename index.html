(FAQ)

Good questions.

常见问题
How do I get a quotation?如何获取报价？

WhatsApp us a photo of the location, the approximate size and what you have in mind — or use the form below. We'll reply with ideas and a quotation. 发送现场照片、大概尺寸和需求，我们会尽快回复设计建议与报价。

When can I contact you?什么时候可以联系？

Anytime — we're open 24 hours, 7 days a week. Many F&B installs are done after closing so your stall doesn't lose business. 我们全天24小时营业，可在您收档后安装，不影响生意。

Do you install islandwide?全岛都可以安装吗？

Yes — hawker centres, food courts, malls, shophouses and offices across Singapore. 是的，我们提供全岛安装服务。

I don't have a design or logo. Can you help?没有设计稿可以吗？

Of course. We design signage and menu boards from scratch, including bilingual English / Chinese layouts. 当然可以，我们提供中英文双语设计。

Do you repair or maintain existing signs?可以维修旧招牌吗？

Yes — we maintain signboards and lightboxes. Send us a photo of the problem and we'll advise. 可以，请发送照片给我们评估。

How long does a project take?制作需要多久？

It depends on size and materials. We confirm the timeline with your quotation — tell us if you have an opening date to meet. 视尺寸和材料而定，报价时会确认交期。
